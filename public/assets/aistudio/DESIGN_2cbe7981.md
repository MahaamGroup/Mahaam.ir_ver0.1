## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an accessible, corporate-grade institutional foundation for executive advisory, high-level organizational decision support, and strategic global mobility. It pairs the signature corporate blue directly derived from the core identity with warm ivory card surfaces, deep midnight grounding, and dependable typography.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate & Bespoke Editorial
The aesthetic avoids transactional urgency, flashing sales tropes, and juvenile gamification. Instead, it synthesizes the precision and craftsmanship of contemporary editorial product interfaces (Stripe, Linear, Apple) with institutional credibility:
- **Discreet Authority**: Visuals convey calm confidence, confidentiality, and measured strategic foresight.
- **Warm Editorial Canvas**: Rather than stark, sterile whites, resting surfaces leverage warm ivory tones to foster focus during complex decision evaluation.
- **RTL & Latin Symbiosis**: Tailored for Persian RTL reading flow while seamlessly framing technical Latin designations and corporate identities.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a primary corporate blue (`#005F9E`), supported by a secondary botanical lime accent (`#65A30D`) extracted directly from the corporate identity, framed against midnight foundational layers (`#0A1128`) and a warm ivory canvas (`#FAF9F6`).

### Functional Color Applications
- **Primary Brand Anchor (`#005F9E`)**: Used for authoritative primary action targets, key brand emblems, active link indicators, and primary focus boundaries.
- **Secondary Natural Accent (`#65A30D`)**: Applied with strict discipline for validated path indicators, progress completion points, and executive milestone badges.
- **Tertiary Deep Navy (`#0A1128`)**: Forms structural foundations, hero canopies, and high-contrast night sections where deep concentration and discreet confidentiality are required.
- **Warm Ivory Surface (`#FAF9F6`)**: The primary daytime foundation. Softens visual strain and establishes an inviting, editorial publication tone.
- **Neutral Boundaries & Text**:
  - Slate Dark (`#0F172A`): Primary high-contrast text on ivory canvas.
  - Slate Muted (`#64748B`): Contextual annotations, metadata, and pathway step coordinates.
  - Border Hairline: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` on light surfaces; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` across dark containers.

## Typography

The typography scale harmonizes the clean, modern warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the functional clarity and legibility of **Inter**. When deployed in Persian RTL contexts, the system seamlessly interfaces with font stacks such as `"IRANSans"` or `"Vazirmatn"`, applying identical rhythmic proportions and line-height multipliers.

### Structural Typographic Rules
- **Proportionate Leading**: Generous line-heights prevent clipping of complex Persian diacritics and ligatures while giving dense strategic analysis breathing room.
- **RTL Directional Discipline**: Persian prose and structural markers align strictly to the right (`text-align: right`). Forced justification (*kashida*) is strictly avoided to preserve typographical harmony.
- **Hierarchy & Text Measure**: Editorial reading lines are constrained to a measure of 58–68 characters (~640px) to maintain optimal scanning ease across executive briefs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined, architectural 12-column fluid grid system bounded by an executive maximum width of **1320px**. 

### Spacing & Rhythmic Intervals
- **Base Grid Unit**: 4px/8px incremental base rhythm. Layout modules strictly adhere to this cadence to deliver optical calm and rhythm.
- **Outer Canvas Margins**: 
  - Mobile (<640px): `1.25rem` (20px)
  - Tablet (641px–1024px): `2.5rem` (40px)
  - Desktop (>1024px): `4rem` (64px)
- **Section Separation**: High vertical spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl` / 64px–96px) demarcates distinct strategic chapters, replacing noisy visual dividers with purposeful whitespace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **tonal surface layering, coordinate precision lines, and subtle ambient shadows**, avoiding thick or unrefined drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Planes
1. **Foundation Level (Level 0)**: Warm Ivory (`#FAF9F6`) or Nocturnal Midnight (`#0A1128`).
2. **Elevated Card Layer (Level 1)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) on light mode; Slate Navy (`#0F172A`) on dark mode. Bounded by an ultra-fine 1px border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus (Level 2)**: Crisp surfaces with soft ambient drop shadows (`0 12px 32px rgba(8, 21, 37, 0.06)`), complemented by micro frosted glass backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
4. **Interactive Hover States**: Refined 2px to 4px vertical lift accompanied by a diffuse, low-opacity glow (`0 8px 24px rgba(0, 95, 158, 0.15)`), signifying responsiveness without disrupting layout stability.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape standard, expressing structural precision, warmth, and contemporary polish without drifting into juvenile pill shapes.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Radii (`rounded` - 0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard input fields, segment selectors, and small tags.
- **Large Structural Radii (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px)**: Decision buttons, interactive audience cards, and dialog windows.
- **Extra-Large Radii (`rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px)**: Major section containers, executive journey panels, and primary interactive showcase modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Styled with corporate blue (`#005F9E`) surface, crisp white text (`#FAF9F6`), `rounded-lg` geometry, and a 1px inner highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Features subtle 2px elevation on hover, accompanied by RTL directional arrows gliding outward (`transform: translateX(-4px)`).
- **Secondary Button**: Translucent warm surface on dark containers or pure white on light canvas, paired with a 1px border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`). Border transitions to `#005F9E` on hover.
- **Touch Bounds**: Generous touch footprint with a minimum height of 48px to satisfy enterprise accessibility guidelines.

### Cards & Decision Framework Modules
- **Audience Recognition Cards**: Tailored for distinct user cohorts (e.g., Professionals, Executives, Business Owners, Organizations). Built with `rounded-xl` contours, soft white foundations, hairline borders, and generous internal padding (`space-xl`).
- **Interactive Framework Panels**: Showcase decision stages with sequential hairline progress lines that softly illuminate with the secondary lime accent (`#65A30D`) as milestones are achieved.

### Inputs & Executive Assessments
- **Field Styling**: Form inputs utilize subtle warm neutral surfaces (`#F8FAFC`) with clear 1px borders.
- **Persistent Labels**: Labels are permanently visible above the input field in semi-bold weight (`label-md`), accompanied by supportive contextual hints.
- **Focus Rings**: Smooth, high-contrast outer ring in corporate blue (`#005F9E`) with a 2px offset.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills**: Compact, refined chips (`label-sm`) featuring low-opacity tinted backgrounds and high-contrast text (e.g., lime-tinted background for confirmed evaluation paths, slate-tinted for informational categories).
- **Border**: 1px subtle matching border, keeping tags sharp and legible.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Selection Controls**: 20px structured containers with 4px corner radii for checkboxes and circular geometry for radio selectors. Active state fills with `#005F9E`, displaying an optical white checkmark or centered coordinate pip.
